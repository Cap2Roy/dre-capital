The MAO Formula
MAO = (ARV × 0.70) − Repairs − Fee
ARV
After-repair value. Median $/sqft of renovated solds within 0.5–1 mi, last 3–6 months.
Repairs
Light $20-30/sqft · Cosmetic+ $35-55 · Gut $70-100+. Plus big-five: roof, HVAC, foundation, repipe, panel.
Fee
Your assignment fee. Default $15k. Never shrink to zero — that's how you work for free.
