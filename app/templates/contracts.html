The Rules
  • Disclose you are selling the contract, not the house. Put it in writing. In Texas this keeps you legal without a license.
  • Close through an investor-friendly title company that handles assignments and double closings.
  • Send to top five buyers first, matched by buy box. Then the wider list, then a marketplace.
  • Momentum dies overnight — the day you sign, it goes to your top buyers.
